## Working agreements

* `main` is protected. Every change arrives through a pull request with one review.
* Branch names follow `sprint-<n>/us-<id>-<short-slug>`.
* Commit messages name the requirement and close the issue: `US-3: ... (closes #12)`.
* Each member writes their own bio, self-reflection and contribution statement.
* Weekly evidence is recorded in `docs/process/weekly-evidence.md` by the owner of
  that week, with issue, pull request and commit links.
* If a member cannot finish a task, they say so at the Wednesday check instead of
  making an empty commit to look active.
